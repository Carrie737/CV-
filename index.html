02 / Research & Publications

Reading the market
through its records.

Two articles in Art Market, examining an artist’s auction history and the changing practices of auction houses.

May 2025 · Art Market First author

She Is Unmistakably Herself: An Analysis of the Auction Market for Berthe Morisot’s Works

Analysed historical auction catalogues and market-price data to trace the auction performance of Berthe Morisot’s works from the 1960s to the 21st century.

Compared media, auction houses, geographic markets and price trends to examine changes in the market recognition of her works.

Explore the interactive article ↗
January 2023 · Art Market Third author

Those Green-Cover Auction Catalogues of the 1960s–1970s

Examined 159 historical auction catalogues from Sotheby’s and Christie’s: 108 from Sotheby’s and 51 from Christie’s.

Analysed catalogue design, auction rules, commission structures and pricing information to explore changes in auction-house practices and the development of the modern art auction market.
